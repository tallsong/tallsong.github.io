## 3. Content & Tone Guidelines
- **Voice**: Pragmatic, technically rigorous, approachable, and authentic. Avoid generic AI fluff or overhyped buzzwords.
- **Project Case Studies (`_projects/`)**:
  - Always use the **STAR method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
  - Include architecture diagrams (Mermaid.js preferred), concrete metrics (latency, throughput, memory reduction), and tech stack tables.
- **Blog Posts (`_posts/`)**:
  - Technical posts should solve concrete problems or explain non-obvious engineering trade-offs.
  - Personal essays should reflect genuine reflections on career growth, communication, and learning.
- **Calls to Action (CTA)**: End articles and projects with a natural conversation starter (e.g., inviting feedback, questions, or collaboration).

---

## 4. Technical Constraints & Rules for the Agent
- **Jekyll Front Matter**: Every post and project must have valid YAML front matter (`title`, `date`, `permalink`, `tags`).
- **File Organization**:
  - Articles: `_posts/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`
  - Portfolio items: `_projects/slug.md`
  - Pages: `_pages/slug.md`
- **Design Integrity**: Keep layouts clean, fast-loading, and mobile-friendly. Avoid heavy third-party scripts that compromise user experience.
